# Europe after Napoleon
- Congress of Vienna (1814):
  - After Napoleon lost, the victorious countries came together
  - Aim: Redraw europe's borders
  - Principles: Minimise the threat posed by France
- Results:
  - Creation of buffer states (belgium, netherlands) between france & the rest of europe
  - France lost its conquests (mostly not francophone areas)
