# <span style="color:#fabd2f">Ideologies in the 19th century

## <span style="color:#fabd2f">Liberalism
- Liberty
- Some new ideas from the _`Declaration of The Rights of Men and Citizens`_:
  - Freedom of Religion
  - Freedom of Opinion/Conscience
  - Freedomm of Speech/Press
  - Equality before the law
  - The only limit to individual liberty is the liberty of another person
### Liberal ideal of government
- Constitution
- Free elections

## <span style="color:#fabd2f">Nationalism
- Unites people behind the idea of a 'nation' of common language
- Nation: People of a common language, culture, set of rights

## <span style="color:#fabd2f"> Conservatism
- Reaction to liberalism
- Main ideas are regarding the `preservation of political structures`
