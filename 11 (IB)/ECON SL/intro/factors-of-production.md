# <span style="color:#fabd2f"> Factors of production

### <span style="color:#fabd2f">Raw materials (land)
- Natural resources as well as energy belong here
- All production processes require these resources

### <span style="color:#fabd2f">Labour
- The ability to spend time and effort working towards/on something

### <span style="color:#fabd2f">Capital
- The resoures required to begin an enterprise
- Can be:
  - Money
  - Equipment
  - Workers
  - Buildings
  - Technology
- These resources are invested in hope of extracting a positive ROI (return on investment)

### <span style="color:#fabd2f">Entrepreneur 
- The person responsible for coordinating the use of the other three factors

## <span style="color:#fabd2f">Example -- Opening a bakery
- `Raw materials:`
  - Eggs
  - Flour
  - Water
  - Sugar
- `Labour`:
  - Delivery
  - Cleaning
  - Baking
- `Capital`:
  - Bakery building
  - Oven
  - Kitchen equipment
- `Entrepreneur`:
  - The business-owner/manager
