# <span style="color:#fabd2f">Allocative efficiency
### <span style="color:#fabd2f">Basic definition:
 -  `Occurs when`: Q<sub>supplied</sub> = Q<sub>demanded</sub>

### <span style="color:#fabd2f">Definition with marginal cost/benefit
- `Occurs when`:
  - [marginal benefit](#marginal-benefit) = [marginal cost](#marginal-cost)
  - Alternatively: The point at the `intersection of the MC and the MB` line
#
### Marginal benefit
- The decrease in satisfaction for every unit of a product `consumed`
- Can be illustrated the same way as a `demand curve`
#
### Marginal Cost
- The increase in production cost for every unit of a product `produced`
- Can be illustrated the same way a `supply curve`
