# <span style="color:#fabd2f">The role of the price mechanism

## Price mechanism:
  - `Tweaking the price` of a product to eliminate a `shortage` or a `surplus`

## <span style="color:#fabd2f">Economic Signal, Incentive, Reallocation signal

|Price change|Signal|Incentive|Reallocation|
|-|-|-|-|
|Increase|Shortage|<table><tr><td>Producer</td><td>Q<sub>s</sub>↑</td></tr><tr><td>Consumer</td><td>Q<sub>d</sub>↓</td></tr></table>|More resources toward product A|
|Decrease|Surplus|<table><tr><td>Producer</td><td>Q<sub>s</sub>↓</td></tr><tr><td>Consumer</td><td>Q<sub>d</sub>↑</td></tr></table>|Less resources toward product A|


## <span style="color:#fabd2f">How to decide who gets the product?

### Price-rationing
- People who can pay for a product can buy it

### Non-price rationing
- Systems:
  - Coupon system (=> Coupons can be exchanged for the product)
  - First come-first serve (=> The first person willing to buy the product will get it)
