# 3 cartoon analysis


## 1st cartoon
### People
- Shows three american politicians (maybe people working in congress)
- They are talking about regulating A.I and how they should do it
- One of the congressmen is proposing asking ChatGPT about how they should regulate it

### Physical objects
- A laptop is on one of the congressmen's desks, which the person is trying to use to communicate with chatGPT
- There is an American Flag in the background

### Technique
- Symbolic style
- Straightforward depiction

### Debatable issue
- The proliferation of genAI in decision making/legislation/politics


### Humor technique
- They are trying to decide how to regulate ai using AI --> Situational irony

### Artist's purpose
- Artist is against genAI --> With this cartoon he's trying to convince the reader that the AI problem has gotten as bad as to be helping politicians with decision-making

### personal opinions
- Alexey:
  - Disagrees with such strong negative characterization of the issue
  - agrees that a lot of people are using AI incorrectly/counterproductively
- Bernát:
  - Agrees that a significant number of people are using AI in a counterproductive manner, with some extermists even using it with malicious intent

## 2nd cartoon
### People
- Caveman family of three in the cartoon
- The parents are making cave art
- the kid is sitting on the floor, arms crossed
- The mother says the child isn't making cave art because they are waitig for AI

### Physical objects
- Charcoal used for making the pictographs

### Technique
- Symbolic
- Simplistic

### Debatable issue
- AI again, specifically AI art
- Commenting on the fact that AI images/videos are inherently fake or of inferior quality to human-made art

### Humor technique
- Ironic --> Children are usually very much excited to draw (especially on walls)
- Ironic --> The child will grow up and die way before AI is invented, also they should have no knowledge of AI

### Artist's purpose
- Conveys the message that people are giving up what they enjoy/would enjoy doing for AI to do it instead


### Personal opinions:
- Alexey:
  - Agrees to a certain extent but it depends on the use case and who's using it
- Bernát:
  - Finds it disappointing that people are giving up things they are passionate about for the sake of letting ai do those things instead
