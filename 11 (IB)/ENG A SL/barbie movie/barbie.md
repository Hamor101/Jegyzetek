## <span style="color:#fabd2f">Task -- Collect examples of satire from the barbie movie
- `Humor:`
    - The film begins with a parody of 2001: A space odyssey[^1]

- `Exaggeration:`
  - Sasha calls Barbie a fascist
  - All the barbies reacting to the original barbie's feet turning flat

- `Situational Irony:`
  - Barbie is offered a matrix-esque choice about whether she wants to go back to her old life, but the choice is false; she can't

- `Dramatic Irony:`
  - Before she sets off, all the barbies talk about how barbie will be a hero in the real world and about how she will meet many poweful women.

- `Verbal Irony:`
  - The starting song playing the second day with the same cheerful tune but with lyrics like 'could it be those irrepressible thoughts of death?'


## <span style="color:#fabd2f"> Types of irony:
### <span style="color:#fabd2f">Dramatic Irony
- When the reader or audience knows something that the character does not


### <span style="color:#fabd2f">Verbal Irony
- When the speaker says something different from what they mean. The reality of the situation is often the opposite of what the speaker is saying

### <span style="color:#fabd2f">Situational
- When an event or outcome takes palce that is the opposite of what a person expected
