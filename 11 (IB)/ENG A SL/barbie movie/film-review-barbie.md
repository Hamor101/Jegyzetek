# Film Review - Barbie movie

## Conventions of writing film reviews
- `Voice/POV:`
  - Should engage the reader, have personality
  - Can have humor/sarcasm
- `Plot Summary:`
  - Needs to reveal some of the story
- `Background information:`
  - Can often include special fun facts about production
- `Character descriptions/Actor evaluation:`
  - Reviewers often profile characters
  - Evaluate how they are performed by their actors
- `Director's Body of Work:`
  - Provide information about the director's films
  - Connect the film to them in some way
- `Commentary on Cinematography/Music/Costumes:`
  - Evaluate the cinematic elements/Music
- `Emotive language:`
  - Review can include words with positive or negative connotations based on the reviewers opinion
- `Evaluative language:`
  - The review must evaluate the film's quality
  - Can evaluate:
    - entertainment value
    - social value
    - relevance
    - motivation
- `Comparisons:`
  - Can compare the movie to other works of the same genre favorably or unfavorably
