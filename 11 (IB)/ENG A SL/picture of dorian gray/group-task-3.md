## Comment on the significance of Basil's murder taking place in Chapter 13.
- Perhaps the most significant event in the book
  - The first time Dorian actively chose to take a life, as opposed to just letting other people die
  - This is a person who was present in his early years and meant a lot to him at a time
  - Makes Dorian realize he can basically get away with anything
  - Dorian loses a bit of his sanity --> Essentially gets completely deetached from the situtation
  - He is tortured by his conscience
  - The murder happens with no prelude, Dorian just kills Basil with no escalation
