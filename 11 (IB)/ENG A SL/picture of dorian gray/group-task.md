## What is the relationship between Basil and Dorian? Why doesn't Basil want to exhibit the painting of Dorian?
- Veeeeeeeeery good friends
- Dorian is Basil's muse
- Dorian doesn't know anything about Basil's feelings, feels exhibiting the painting might expose them to the world
- Basil is scared that people will see his personality through the painting
- Basil feels his views will be judged, thinks that artists should avoid expressing themselves too much in their art
