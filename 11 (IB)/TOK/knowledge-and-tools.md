# <span style="color:#fabd2f">Methods and tools of knowledge acquisition

## <span style="color:#fabd2f">Cognitive tools
  - Senses --> We can use our senses to observe and gather experiences
  - Memory --> We can store previously learned information
  - Imagination --> Can be used to make mental models
  - Emotion --> Useful for reinforcement learning, can be used to gauge interest
  - Reason --> Can be used for deduction
  - Language --> Can be used to express/convey knowledge
  - Faith --> Serves as a motivator
  - Intuition --> Speeds up the process of acquiring knowledge

### <span style="color:#fabd2f">Role of cognitive tools in producing knowledge
- What shapes our personal experience of the world?
  - Senses
  - Emotion
  - Community
- What would happen if we tried to isolate just one cognitive tool?
  - Even when trying to make purely rational decisions, out emotions play a role
- How can cognitive tools be misleading?
  - Emotions can be misleading
  - Memory is unreliable
- How do communities of knowers manage our cognitive tools?
  - Shared language/faith

## <span style="color:#fabd2f"> What are the parts of an argument?
- Parts of an argument:
  - Conclusion: What am I stating?
  - Reason: How do i justify the conclusion?
  - Assumption: What must be true so that the whole argument makes sense?
- E.g: I am really healthy now because i lost weight
  - `Conclusion`: I am healthy now
  - `Reason`: I lost weight

## <span style="color:#fabd2f"> Logical fallacies
- Fallacies arise from having `incorrect assumptions`
