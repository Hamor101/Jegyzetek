# <span style="color:#fabd2f"> Knowledge and the knower

## <span style="color:#fabd2f">Facts, claims, truth
- `Fact`: the state in which things actually are, whether or not it's known
- `Claim`: statement about what `might be a fact`
- `Truth`: a claim which has been proven/accepted true

### Map projections
- the 3D earth cannot be accurately projected onto a 2D map
- We must make `compromises between accuracy and usefulness`, based on what we wish to use the map for

- Knowledge works in the same way
  - e.g when explaining something to a child, detail is sacrificed in the interest of understanding


## <span style="color:#fabd2f">Opinions, beliefs, knowledge
### Opinions
- Subjective
- taste-based
- Individual truth (not general)

### Beliefs
- Claims about a general truth
- Could be supported by evidence
  - More objective than opinions
- May/May not be true

### Knowledge
- `Proven`
  - Outcome of experiments/discoveries
- Representation of a general truth about the world
- Any fact can be knowledge

## <span style="color:#fabd2f">What makes claims justified?
- Correspondence theory:
  - If the claim `corresponds with facts` about the world
- Coherence theory:
  - If the claim is `coherent with all other true claims`
- Works in practice
- Correct more often than chance would allow (better than just guessing)
- Helps with:
  - deciding what we want to accept
  - convincing others

## <span style="color:#fabd2f"> Propositional and ability knowledge
### Propositional knowledge ("knowing that"):
  - Body of knowledge of a subject
  - Theories
  - Facts
  - Results of a process/method of deriving truths
  - Rules of a sport/Knowledge about techniques of a sport
### Ability knowledge ("Knowing how"):
  - Language
  - Executing techniques in of sport
  - Skills/Abilities
  - Playing an instrument

## <span style="color:#fabd2f">Communities of knowers
- Sometimes membership critera exist (beliefs, exams, money, auditions)
- Distinct roles within the community (experts/apprentices)
- Usually shared:
  - Beliefs
  - Theories
  - Experiences
  - Ideas
- Knowers are related to such communities
### Experts
- Reliability depends on:
  - Experience
  - Active engagement with the subject over a long period of time
